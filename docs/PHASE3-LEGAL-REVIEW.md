The revised pages avoid claiming secure erasure, universal compatibility, guaranteed error messages during a browser crash, fixed compression ratios or processing times. Policy links identify provider information without claiming that every provider default matches the owner's account. Any future legal additions require a concrete owner-approved draft before publication.
